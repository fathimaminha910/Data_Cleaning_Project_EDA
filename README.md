Exploratory Data Analysis & Statistical Insights

Project Overview

Perform comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Online Retail Dataset to uncover hidden business patterns.

Dataset

- Online Retail Dataset
- Total Records: 534,129
- Total Columns: 11

Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- SciPy
- Google Colab

Implementation Steps

1. Descriptive Statistics

- Calculated Mean
- Median
- Standard Deviation
- Quartiles

2. Data Visualization

- Correlation Heatmap
- Total Sales Histogram
- Total Sales Boxplot
- Outlier Detection

3. Business Hypothesis Testing

- Tested relationship between Quantity and Total Sales.
- Analyzed monthly sales differences.
- Analyzed country-wise sales differences.
- Used statistical tests including correlation and Kruskal-Wallis test.

4. Key Findings

- Quantity and Total Sales have a strong positive correlation.
- Monthly sales show significant differences.
- Country-wise sales show significant differences.
- November recorded the highest monthly sales.
- United Kingdom recorded the highest country-wise sales.

Conclusion

EDA helped identify important sales trends, relationships, and statistical insights from the Online Retail Dataset.
